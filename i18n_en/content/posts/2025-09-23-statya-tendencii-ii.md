My second article was published in the journal "Financial Management" (no. 10, 2025) — "Current trends in the use of artificial intelligence in corporate governance during digital transformation".

## What it's about

I mapped how the role of AI in company management is changing, based on materials from leading global consultancies and Russian academic publications. AI agents are among the article's keywords: by 2025 it was already clear that the next step after chatbots is agents that perform tasks themselves.

## Trends

- Generative AI is becoming a strategic transformation tool — to the point that companies build their own models.
- Adaptive risk management is growing: AI regulation follows a risk-based approach.
- AI is built into strategy at the level of the whole corporation, not individual departments.
- More and more attention goes to the transparency of AI decisions and stakeholder trust.

The article also describes key problems of AI in corporate governance and proposes my model of AI's impact on corporate governance during digital transformation.
