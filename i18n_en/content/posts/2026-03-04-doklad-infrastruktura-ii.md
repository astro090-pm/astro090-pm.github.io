On 4 March 2026 I presented online at the CIV International Scientific and Practical Conference "Current Issues in Economics and Modern Management" (organised in Novosibirsk). The topic: "Technology requirements for company infrastructure when implementing artificial intelligence".

## Main idea

Infrastructure is the precondition for AI to work in management at all — not just a task for IT. Feasibility is defined by four things: data readiness, integration with systems, reliable computing and people.

## Five blocks of requirements

1. **Data and its quality** — shared metric definitions, clear data lineage and accountable owners.
2. **Compute and software base** — stable infrastructure, model version control, assigned maintenance.
3. **Integration with corporate systems** — a model's output only matters when it's built into planning, control and execution.
4. **Data protection and reliability** — access control and stable operation directly affect management's trust in AI.
5. **People** — without staff who maintain models, adoption doesn't scale.

## Typical mistakes

Inconsistent metrics, poor source data, models without an "owner" and a gap with corporate systems. The result is always the same: management stops trusting AI and goes back to manual procedures.
