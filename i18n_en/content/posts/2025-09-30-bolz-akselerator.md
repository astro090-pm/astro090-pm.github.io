From June to September 2025 we went through the Moscow Innovation Cluster's "Academy of Innovators" accelerator with bolz.tech — an ecosystem of sharing services in one window. I'm a co-founder and the CPO.

## The product

Scooters, bikes, cars, power banks — every sharing service has its own app. According to our data, in 60% of cases the service installed on your phone isn't nearby. bolz embeds into partner apps via WebView: you can rent any sharing service without installing another app or signing up, and one card works for all.

By the accelerator we already had partnership acceptance from MTS Urent and GetEnergy and a power-bank sharing service running in production.

## What the accelerator gave us

- Product packaging: the problem from three sides — user, sharing service, partner app.
- Hypothesis testing: a survey of 200 users across Russia and Moscow and unit economics for two stages — "first 100 paying users" and product-market fit.
- A pitch to experts and their feedback.

## "Moscow Innovator 2025"

In parallel, in July–August, we took part in the "Moscow Innovator 2025" contest with bolz.tech.

## What came next

Working on bolz.tech, we saw users increasingly arrive via AI assistants rather than apps. That's how [bolz.dev](bolz-dev-mcp.html) appeared — an MCP connector for real-world actions by AI agents.
