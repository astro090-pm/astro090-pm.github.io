On 10 September 2026 — a talk at the XVI International Scientific and Practical Conference "Development of Modern Science and Education: Current Issues, Achievements and Innovations" (Penza): "Organisational readiness of Russian companies to adopt artificial intelligence in the management system: barriers and directions of development".

It's my third talk of 2026. The first two were about infrastructure and risks; this one is about organisation: what prevents a company from adopting AI in management even when the technology is there, and where to go next.
