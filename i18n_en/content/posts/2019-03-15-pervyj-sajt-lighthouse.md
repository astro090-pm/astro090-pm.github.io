Before product management I was an engineer selling industrial equipment. My first "product" was the website of Lighthouse Technologies, where I worked as a business development manager.

## The problem

The company supplied equipment for the power, oil and gas, chemical and food industries. Customers were plant engineers and buyers, deals were long, and there was no proper website where one could understand the range and leave a request.

## What I did

- Defined the concept and structure of the new site: product lines, catalogue, news, request contacts.
- Designed the functionality and filled the site with content myself.
- After launch, kept developing the functionality and ran the news feed.
- Connected Yandex Metrica and analysed how customers behaved on the site.

In parallel I rolled out Bitrix24 in the company and took part in optimising business processes and logistics.

## Why it matters

Back then I didn't know words like "backlog" or "CustDev", but I was doing product work: understanding who the user is and what they need, building a solution, launching it and looking at the data. A few months later I moved to systems analysis at Concern GRANIT, and from there into product management.
