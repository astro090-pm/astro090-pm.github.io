My third peer-reviewed article was published — "Risks and limitations of using artificial intelligence in company management" in "Economics and Management: Problems, Solutions" (2025, no. 10). It's the topic closest to my job: I build a service with an AI agent at a bank.

## What it's about

I examined what a company risks when it uses AI in management, using banks as the example. Three groups of constraints: regulatory and ethical, technological and organisational, institutional and staffing.

## What's new in it

- Systematised the Bank of Russia's positions on AI risks.
- Proposed treating ethics as a "soft law" mechanism.
- Formulated a criterion of a bank's technological maturity as a condition for safe AI adoption.
- Proposed an adapted classification of AI risks in management that unites several existing approaches.
- Built a model of how these risks affect each other.

## Why it matters for a product manager

AI risks aren't just for lawyers. Model risk, data quality and accountability for a model's decision are product questions: they decide whether users will trust the service.
