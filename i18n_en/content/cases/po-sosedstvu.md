# Po Sosedstvu: taking an offline grocery store online

**Co-founder & Product Manager** · Po Sosedstvu · August 2022 — November 2024 (side project)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Product** | online store for Belarusian groceries with home and office delivery, web + adaptive (B2C and B2B) |
| **Website** | [po-sosedstvu.ru](https://po-sosedstvu.ru/) — live since 2022 |
| **Stage** | built from scratch |
| **Stack** | 1C-Bitrix, 1C |
| **Key result (B2B)** | orders **+89%**, average check **+71%**, revenue **+58%** |

## Context

An offline store needed an online channel for consumers and corporate clients. Resources were small, so the product was built on an off-the-shelf platform with external contractors.

## What I did

- Created the concept and the offline-to-online strategy.
- Designed mockups.
- Designed online and offline business processes.
- Assembled a team of contractors (design, development, 1C, analytics, SEO) and set up the delivery process.
- Launched the store for B2C and B2B customers.

## How the store works today

- **Assortment:** pantry, dairy and cheese, deli, fish, frozen, sweets, drinks; curated lists "Great price", "Customers' choice", "New this month".
- **B2C delivery:** Moscow, Khimki and Dolgoprudny; next-day delivery, free pickup point.
- **B2B channel:** application form → document check → personal account with prices and stock; minimum order 15,000 ₽, free delivery in Moscow region, shipping to other regions.
- **Trust and retention:** certificates on request, money-back guarantee, loyalty points.
