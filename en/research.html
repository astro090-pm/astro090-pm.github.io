Research

I study how artificial intelligence changes the way a company is managed: not individual tasks, but the system itself — planning, decision-making, control and communication. It's a continuation of my product work: by day I build a service with an AI agent at a bank, and in my research I try to understand why such projects work in some companies and fail in others.

How I do research

Besides reviewing the literature and company practice, the empirical part of the dissertation is a case study at a large Russian bank. I ran a series of semi-structured interviews with four groups of respondents: AI product and project managers, specialists from the AI technology centre, top management, and employees who work with AI tools every day. The interviews are anonymised, and the answers are compiled into a thematic matrix: where AI is built into management, how goals and KPIs for adoption are set, what gets in the way and what helps. The summary is in the post “What holds AI back in management”.

Questions I'm interested in

  • In which management processes AI really makes a difference, and where it's just hype.
  • What risks and limitations it brings — from data quality to accountability for a decision.
  • What a company needs to prepare in advance: data, infrastructure, people and rules.
