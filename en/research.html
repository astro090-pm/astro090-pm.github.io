Research

I study how artificial intelligence changes corporate management — not individual tasks but the system itself: planning, decision-making, control and communication. It complements my product work: by day I build a service with an AI agent at a bank, and in research I look at why such projects succeed in some companies and fail in others.

PhD programme, RANEPA · 2024–2027 · Institute of Industry Management

“Using artificial intelligence to improve a company's management system”

3peer-reviewed articles (VAK list)
3international conference talks

Method

Besides literature and industry-practice analysis, the empirical part is a case study in a large Russian bank: semi-structured interviews with AI product and project managers, AI centre specialists, top management and employees who use AI tools daily. The interviews are anonymised and coded into a thematic matrix.
