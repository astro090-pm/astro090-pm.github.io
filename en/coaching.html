My path into coaching

Status: Planned

This page complements the site's main line — products and AI. Here I'll openly document my path into business coaching: learning, practice and reflection, guided by the professional standards of the International Coaching Federation (ICF).

Important: I don't claim to hold an ICF credential and I don't offer coaching services on this site. The stages below are a plan; each will be marked as done only with a date and evidence.

Why I'm doing this

I've led teams of up to 50 people, and now I teach an IT Management course to first-year students. The coaching approach — asking questions instead of handing out ready answers — matters both in product management and in teaching. I want to learn it systematically, to professional standards.

Route

The target is ICF's first level, Associate Certified Coach (ACC). Requirements are taken from the official ICF website as of October 2026; I re-check them before each step.

  1. 1

    Choose a training programme Planned

    ICF-accredited (Level 1 or Level 2) or one with Continuing Coach Education hours — this determines the application path

  2. 2

    Coach training Planned

    At least 60 hours

  3. 3

    Practice Planned

    100 hours of coaching with clients, at least 75 of them paid

  4. 4

    Mentor coaching Planned

    10 hours with an experienced coach over at least three months

  5. 5

    Performance evaluation and exam Planned

    Evaluation of a recorded session and the ICF ACC exam

  6. 6

    ACC application Planned

    Submitted to ICF once all requirements are met

Three different things that are easy to confuse

  • TrainingA course or programme someone completed. It shows knowledge gained, not a qualification.
  • Programme certificateA school's document confirming completion. It is not an ICF qualification, even if the programme is ICF-accredited.
  • ICF credentialAn individual professional qualification (ACC, PCC, MCC) awarded by ICF itself after training, practice, mentor coaching, evaluation and an exam.
