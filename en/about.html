About me

Hi 👋 This page is a short manual for working with me. It's for colleagues, teammates and anyone I'm starting something with: so we spend less time getting used to each other and more time on the product.

What I do

By day I'm a product manager, and as a PhD student at RANEPA I'm writing a dissertation on “Using artificial intelligence to improve a company's management system”. It's the same topic from two sides: at work I build a service with an AI agent for credit analysis, and in my research I look at how AI changes not individual tasks but management itself — planning, decision-making, control and communication inside a company.

I'm interested in three questions:

  • in which management processes AI really makes a difference, and where it's just hype;
  • what risks and limitations it brings — from data quality to accountability for a decision;
  • what a company needs to prepare in advance: data, infrastructure, people and rules.

That's what my articles and talks are about, and I cover the practical side in the AI agents section.

What I believe in

  • Hypothesis → test → decision
  • One primary metric per experiment
  • Business results matter more than the number of features
  • AI strengthens management but doesn't take responsibility away from people
  • Process and data first, the model second
  • AI where it delivers measurable value, not AI for its own sake

How to work with me

I start with “why”. Before discussing a solution, I'll ask which user or business problem we're solving and how we'll know we've solved it. It's not distrust, it's a habit.

I like numbers and facts. An opinion without data is a fine starting point, but I want to make decisions based on customer interviews, analytics or an experiment.

Sober about AI. If you suggest “adding AI”, I'll ask which process it will improve, how we'll measure the effect and who is responsible when the model is wrong. If there are answers, I'm the first to support it.

Feedback — directly. If something is wrong with the product, the process or my work, tell me right away and to the point. I give feedback the same way: specifically, with a suggestion for what to change.

Bad news — early. A missed deadline I learn about a week ahead is plan B. The same deadline I learn about on release day is a fire.

Communication. I reply fastest on Telegram. I read email, but not instantly. If it's urgent, say so.

Meetings. A meeting needs a goal and an outcome: at the end we write down who does what and by when.

If you're unsure whether to message me — message me.
