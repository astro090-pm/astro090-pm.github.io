User jobs (JTBD)

  • 🛴User

    When there is no scooter or power bank nearby from the service whose app I have, I want to rent from another one without a new install or sign-up, so that I don't waste time on yet another app.

  • 🔋Sharing service

    When the promotion budget is small, I want to get customers from other apps with large audiences, so that I grow without my own marketing.

  • 📱Partner app

    When I need to raise MAU and usage frequency, I want to add 24/7 sharing to the app, so that users come back more often and the audience is monetized.

Problem

  • Users: in 60% of cases the sharing service installed on your phone isn't nearby — and using another means a new app, sign-up and card.
  • Sharing services: negative unit economics, small marketing budgets, organic-only growth.
  • Partner apps: looking for ways to raise MAU, frequency and loyalty, and to monetise users.

Solution

bolz embeds into other apps via WebView: users rent any sharing service without installing a new app or signing up; one card works for all. Up to 30% of users with 24/7 sharing access come back to the host app 2–5 extra times a month. Business model: from 10% of each rental.

What I did as CPO

  • Ran a survey of 200 users in Russia and Moscow.
  • Defined the product strategy and the B2B2C model.
  • Designed the user journey and architecture: bolz web app inside partner apps, single backend, API connections to sharing services, business dashboard.
  • Modelled unit economics for "first 100 paying users" and product-market fit (CAC 500–1,000 ₽, D30 retention 3–7%, LTV 3–7k ₽).
  • Owned the roadmap: from power banks to bikes, scooters, cars, coworking and EV charging.

Technical decisions

How to reach users

Options

  • our own app
  • ✓ a web app inside other apps (WebView)
Why
Users rent any sharing service without a new install or sign-up, and we grow without our own marketing using partners' audiences.
Trade-off
A long cycle with each partner: six months passed from agreeing to partner to production.
Product role
As CPO I defined the strategy and the B2B2C embedding model.

What to launch first

Options

  • all sharing types at once
  • ✓ one type — power banks
Why
One sharing type is faster to take to production: the power bank service launched in December 2024.
Trade-off
At launch the “everything in one window” value isn't fully visible.
Product role
I ran the roadmap: from power banks to bikes, scooters, cars, coworkings and EV charging.

How to handle payment

Options

  • payment in each sharing service separately
  • ✓ one card link for all sharing services
Why
The user's main pain is signing up and linking a card again; partners get paid instantly via YooKassa.
Trade-off
Bolz takes on settlements with partners.
Product role
I designed the user journey and the architecture.

What I learned

  • Embedding into partners' apps lets you grow without a marketing budget, but the cycle with each partner is long: half a year passed from partnership approval to production.
  • It's better to start with one type of sharing (power banks) than with all of them at once.
  • We noticed users increasingly come through AI assistants — and bolz.dev grew out of that.
