Process automation for a government department (B2G)

Systems Analyst → Product Manager · Concern GRANIT · August 2019 — October 2021
Product web service automating a government department's processes (B2G)
Audience 50 users
Stage built from scratch
Stack Java
Career track lead systems analyst (2019–2020) → product manager (2020–2021)

What I did

As a systems analyst

  • Analysed the client's business processes and designed solutions.
  • Modelled processes in BPMN.
  • Gathered and agreed requirements with the client.
  • Wrote tasks for developers and checked delivery.

As a product manager

  • Worked with clients and stakeholders, researched user needs.
  • Owned the roadmap and backlog.
  • Prepared specifications, user stories and UI mockups.
  • Led the development team.

What this case shows

A strong analytical foundation: moving into product management through systems analysis, requirements and process work in a complex B2G environment.
