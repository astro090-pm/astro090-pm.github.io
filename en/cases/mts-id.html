MTS ID: seamless sign-in across the MTS ecosystem

Key resultintegrated into 9 flagship MTS products and MTS Bank; seamless logins +23%

Role
Product Manager
Where
MTS (largest Russian telecom)
When
October 2021 — May 2023
Product
MTS ID — single seamless sign-in for ecosystem products, web + mobile (B2B2C)
Audience
~10M users
Stage
in production
Stack
Java, Swift, Kotlin

Context

An ecosystem has many products, and every extra login step loses users. MTS ID lets people use all services with one account without signing in again. The product's value grows with every connected service, so the core work is integrations and reliability.

What I did

  • Ran customer development, formulated and tested hypotheses.
  • Defined product metrics.
  • Owned the roadmap and backlog, led the development team.
  • Built and defended the product budget.

Results

  • Integrated seamless sign-in into 9 flagship MTS products and MTS Bank.
  • Together with MTS Pay, integrated sign-in into 2 external products.
  • Moved the product from a monolith to microservices.
  • Seamless logins grew by 23%, seamless authorisations by 7%.

What this case shows

A platform product at scale: working with many internal consumer teams, architectural change (monolith → microservices) and ecosystem-level product metrics.

What I learned

  • A platform's value grows with every connected service, so the main work is integrations with consumer teams, not new features.
  • Moving from a monolith to microservices was part of the product work, not just an engineering task.
  • An ecosystem-level metric — the share of seamless logins — shows a platform's value better than the number of integrations.
