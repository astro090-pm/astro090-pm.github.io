Asia Online: relaunching a grocery retailer's e-commerce

Head of E-commerce (product owner role) · Asia Retail · April 2023 — April 2024
Product online grocery delivery, web + mobile (B2C)
Audience ~600k users
Starting point mobile app rated 1.1 in the App Store
Stack Python, React Native, Flutter
Key result revenue +103%, orders +67%, churn −24%

Context

The retailer's online channel was underperforming: the app had poor ratings, order intake and picking were not set up as processes, and there was no in-house development team. The goal was to turn e-commerce into a real growth channel.

What I did

Strategy and partnerships

  • Built the e-commerce strategy, including an integration with Glovo (the local food-delivery leader in Kyrgyzstan).
  • Launched a storefront inside the M-Bank banking app.
  • Defined the online communications strategy.

Product

  • Refactored and redesigned the Asia Online app and website.
  • Added online payments.
  • Built a "Picker" mobile app for in-store order assembly.
  • Set up analytics collection and incident tracking.

Operations

  • Optimised order intake, processing and picking.
  • Introduced call-centre ordering with customer scripts.
  • Increased the number of stores fulfilling online orders.
  • Automated price, discount and stock updates; expanded the range by 2,000 SKUs.

Team

  • Built an IT product development team and introduced Agile.
  • Restructured the department and introduced KPIs.

Results

Metric Change
Revenue +103%
Share of orders from the app +130%
Orders +67%
New users +44%
App downloads +37%
Active customers +31%
Conversion to purchase +17%
Customer satisfaction +16%
Customer churn −24%
Order cancellations −15%

What this case shows

A full product relaunch where growth came from product (app, payments), operations (picking, call centre, assortment), channels (Glovo, banking app) and team (Agile, KPIs) at the same time.
