Learning material format

Every material follows one template. If code hasn't been tested, that's stated right at the start.

  1. What we're learning
  2. Why an AI Product Engineer needs it
  3. A short explanation
  4. An example or diagram
  5. A practical assignment
  6. Full code, if the assignment involves programming
  7. Run commands and environment requirements
  8. How to check the result
  9. Common mistakes and how to diagnose them
  10. Limitations and further sources
