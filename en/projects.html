Weekend Planner

A Telegram bot that builds an hour-by-hour weekend plan in a minute: it checks the weather, events and places, fits the budget and takes kids' ages into account.

  • Three agents — planner, scout and critic — talk over the A2A protocol
  • Tools live in a custom MCP server; there is a browser agent
  • The model only chooses options — names, prices and times are filled in by code
  • 32 eval cases and product metrics: activation, retention, ratings
