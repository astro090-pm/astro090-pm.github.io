My first peer-reviewed article — how Russian companies use AI in management

31 May 2025journal articleAI in management

My first academic article was published — "Specific features of artificial intelligence use in the management of Russian companies" in the journal "Economics and Management: Problems, Solutions" (2025, vol. 7, no. 6).

What it's about

I examined how Russian companies use AI in management and what stops them from getting value from it, drawing on international and Russian research on AI's impact on management efficiency and on reports about Russian companies' practice.

Key findings

  • Universal off-the-shelf AI solutions poorly reflect the specifics of Russian companies' management tasks.
  • Models that rely on the organisation's internal parameters and its decision-making structure work better.
  • For AI to deliver, algorithms must be built into the system for evaluating management decisions and adapted to the company's resource constraints.

The result is an algorithm for optimising AI use in company management.

Why it matters for a product manager

I see the same in products: "add AI" doesn't work; AI built into a specific decision process with a clear metric does.
