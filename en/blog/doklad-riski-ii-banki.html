Talk on how a bank can reduce AI risks — from accountability to a roadmap

7 September 2026conferenceAI in managementfintech

On 7 September 2026 — an online talk at the CX International Scientific and Practical Conference "Current Issues in Economics and Modern Management" (organised in Novosibirsk): "Organisational and managerial mechanisms for reducing the risks of AI in bank management".

It continues my article on AI risks. There I looked at what a bank risks; here — what exactly to do about it.

Key points

  • Mechanisms, not just threats. Most papers list AI risks; I show which tools a bank can use to reduce them.
  • Seven risk groups are projections of one process. A data error can be a technological, ethical, reputational and regulatory problem at once.
  • The bank is accountable. According to the Bank of Russia, the credit institution — not the model developer — is responsible for correct use of AI.
  • A set of mechanisms: an AI accountability function, model life-cycle management (validation, monitoring, explainability), data and security controls, vendor dependency management, an ethics code and skills development.
  • Four levels of technological maturity — from initial to systemic; each allows a certain depth of AI use, from supporting analytics to strategic decisions with human control retained.
  • A five-step roadmap: model inventory → assigning accountability → validation and monitoring → ethics code and consumer protection → developing people and cross-functional teams.
