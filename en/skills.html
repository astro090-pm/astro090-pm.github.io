AI skills

Skills are instruction packages for an AI agent (Claude Code and compatible tools) that teach it to do a specific job well: a presentation, research, a market analysis, test-driven code. This is my working set — 28 skills grouped by product management tasks.

The set is based on the open collection piofant/skills by Vova Lutsenko, which also includes skills by other authors (superpowers, agent-teams, humanizer-ru, Deep-Research and frontend-design by Anthropic). I gave them my own names based on the task; the “source” link leads to the author.

📊 Presentations and documents 5

  • Dark presentationMinimalist slides on a dark background where every headline is a conclusion, not a topic.source
  • Investor pitchA deck for investors: problem, solution, market, traction, team.source
  • Consulting reportConsulting-style slides, reports and PDFs from plain Markdown.source
  • Product caseA case breakdown from problem statement to a finished presentation.source
  • Academic paperDefences, research talks and reports following Russian academic standards (GOST).source

🔎 Research and analytics 4

  • Deep researchResearch from a blank page to a summary report with sources.source
  • Market analysisA market through the SCPR and MECE frameworks, TAM/SAM/SOM sizing, RICE prioritisation.source
  • NotebookLM notebookWorking with sources in NotebookLM: notes, podcasts, summaries.source
  • Material breakdownBreaks a talk or article into layers: facts, meaning, conclusions, what to do.source

✍️ Writing 3

  • Natural text editorRemoves bureaucratic and “machine” phrasing from Russian text.source
  • Writing in someone's voiceWrites in a specific author's style from samples of their texts.source
  • Academic writingTexts for committees: from a motivation letter to a dissertation defence.source

🗂 Personal context 5

  • Telegram contextPulls the relevant conversation from Telegram chats into the work.source
  • CalendarKnows the week's schedule: meetings, times, places.source
  • Google Drive searchFinds the right file on Drive and brings it into the work.source
  • Semantic blog searchRemembers all posts and finds them by meaning, not by keywords.source
  • Audio transcriptionTranscribes voice messages and meeting recordings locally.source

🤖 Agents and task management 5

  • Finding and installing skillsFinds a suitable skill and connects it right away.source
  • Loop until doneRepeats a task in a loop until it is finished.source
  • Agent teamAssembles a team of agents with reviewers for a single feature.source
  • Structured thinkingThinks a question through slowly and step by step when the answer isn't obvious.source
  • Expert debatePits several experts' positions against each other until they reach a shared conclusion.source

🛠 Development 6

  • Requirements discoveryWon't let coding start until it's clear what we're building and why.source
  • Plan and executeA step-by-step plan, handing tasks to agents and execution.source
  • Test-driven developmentTest first, then code; on failure it looks for the cause, not the symptom.source
  • Code reviewSends code for review, works through comments and gets the branch merged.source
  • Interface designInterfaces with a deliberate visual approach instead of a template.source
  • Creating skillsHelps write a new skill and understand existing ones.source
